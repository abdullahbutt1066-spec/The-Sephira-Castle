The Sephira Castle

These words were set down when the first card was drawn.

First — Of Entry

The Castle admits only those who choose a card. There are twenty-two seats, and no more. Once a seat is taken, it is bound.

Second — Of Names

Your codename is your card. It is your face here. It cannot be changed once chosen.

Third — Of Speech

Speak freely in the Gathering. Your words carry your color and your sigil, and every member will know you by them.

Fourth — Of Private Audience

Some words are not for the Gathering. If you wish to speak privately with another member, you must ask the Fool. He alone opens the doors of private chambers.

Fifth — Of The Fool

The Fool is one seat, held by one person. He is not above the rules — he is their keeper.

Sixth — Of Silence

What is spoken in a private chamber remains there. To break this silence is to break the Club.

Seventh — Of Expulsion

The Fool may strike a name from the record. Such a name is not spoken again, and the one who held it may not return.

The Wheel turns. Choose your card.
